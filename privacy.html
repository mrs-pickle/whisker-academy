Privacy Policy

Meadowlark's World: Whisker Academy

Effective: November 7, 2026

Meadowlark's World: Whisker Academy is a reading game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing about your child.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • Nothing your child does in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your browser's own storage on this device so your child can pick up where they left off:

  • your child's name and the kitten's name, as a grown-up types them in, and any words or key words a grown-up adds
  • lesson progress: which words and sounds have been practiced, how each session went (the date, minutes played and how many answers were right), check and assessment results, the place in the story, and the gifts, crayons and coloring pages earned
  • settings, such as sound on or off, text spacing, background tint, session length and the lesson reached, and the grown-up PIN, which is stored scrambled, not as the PIN itself
  • coloring: your child's coloring of each page, and any pictures a grown-up adds as coloring pages (saved smaller, without the photo's place and time)
  • voice recordings, only if a grown-up makes them in the Grown-Up area
  • a copy of the game's own files, so it works without the internet

When an update changes how the game saves, it may also keep an automatic backup copy of these on the device.

These never leave the device. We cannot see them, and they are not backed up anywhere unless you choose to save a backup file (or a tutor report) yourself in the Grown-Up area; that file goes only where you save it.

The microphone

The game asks for the microphone only when a grown-up chooses to record in the Grown-Up area (Sound check). Recordings are saved on this device and played back only on this device, and go into a backup file only if you save one. Your browser will ask your permission first, and you can say no; the game works without it.

Hosting

The game's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. We do not receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The game is made for children, including children under 13. Because we do not collect, use or share personal information from children, we do not need to ask for parental consent. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent before collecting anything.

Deleting your data

In the Grown-Up area you can erase parts of it yourself:

  • Settings → Start over erases your child's progress (the names, settings, recordings and added pictures stay).
  • Sound check → Delete recording removes a recording.
  • Coloring pages → Delete removes a page and its coloring.
  • Backup → Remove a student erases one student, when two share the device.

To erase everything the game saved, clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under "Site settings" or "Cookies and site data"). Uninstalling the app or removing the home-screen icon may not clear it on every device.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. We will never start collecting personal information without updating this page first.
